# FL-02 — Proof Statement

I can build AI systems that turn model predictions into usable, confidence-aware decision-support outputs. The person I want this to reach is a founder of a small healthtech startup who needs to make risk or triage decisions from imperfect data. The action I want them to take is to hire me for a paid pilot where I can apply this capability to a real problem.

This needs to exist because a CV or LinkedIn profile can describe my AI/ML and software-development skills, but they cannot demonstrate as directly that I can turn a trained model into a working technical solution that produces interpretable outputs for human decision-making.
